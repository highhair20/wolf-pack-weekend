The Trail

Mid-December in the high desert means crisp, sunny days, nights near freezing, and sundown around 4:40. Everything is booked, so all you need to do is pack.

Friday Dec 18 · Sound bath at sundown

  • 12:00 pmWe hit the road east, about two and a half hours into the desert.
  • 3:00 pmWe arrive at the Integratron in Landers and wander the grounds before our session.
  • 3:30 pmSound bath inside the IntegratronWe lie under a wooden dome while 22 quartz crystal bowls are played live. It lasts an hour, and we come out just as the sun goes down.
  • 4:45 pmA quick stop in Yucca Valley to pick up treats for the wolves.
  • 5:45 pmCheck in at the Pioneertown MotelOur room for two nights, on the old Western movie set.
  • 6:30 pmDinner and live music at the Red Dog Saloon, just down Mane Street.Then the stars. Pioneertown is about as dark as skies get this close to LA.

Saturday Dec 19 · Wolves and Willi

  • 8:00 amCoffee at the motel Canteen, then breakfast on the way.
  • 9:00 amA scenic drive north through the desert to Lucerne Valley.
  • 10:00 amWolf Mountain SanctuaryWe meet each wolf, hear its story, and get some hands-on time with one of them. Wolf kisses are allowed. Paw prints on your coat are likely.
  • 12:45 pmLunch at Crossroads Cafe in Joshua Tree, then the little shops of Sun Alley and the World Famous Crochet Museum.
  • 2:30 pmNoah Purifoy's Outdoor Desert Art Museum: 10 acres of strange and wonderful sculpture in the late afternoon light.
  • 4:15 pmBack to the motel to warm up and watch the sun set over Mane Street.
  • 6:30 pmBBQ dinner at Pappy & Harriets.
  • 9:30 pmWilli Carlisle, liveBanjo, fiddle, accordion and story songs in the best little roadhouse in the West. Then we walk back to our room under the stars.

Sunday Dec 20 · Into the park

  • 8:00 amCoffee, then we say goodbye to Pioneertown.
  • 8:30 amBreakfast at Crossroads CafeA big desert breakfast in Joshua Tree town, then just five minutes to the park gate.
  • 9:45 amInto Joshua Tree National Park.
  • 10:15 amHidden Valley and Barker Dam: two easy walks among giant boulders. Bighorn sheep sometimes come down to the water in winter.
  • 12:30 pmSkull Rock and Cap Rock.
  • 2:00 pmJoshua Tree Saloon: burgers and the Sunday open mic on the patioThe half-pound Mineshaft Burger is the house legend.
  • 3:30 pmHome, with sand in our boots.

The Main Events

Landers · Friday

The Integratron

3:30 pm

A 38-foot wooden dome built in the 1950s by George Van Tassel, who believed it could recharge the body. The acoustics are so good that a whisper carries across the room.

Bring a cozy blanket. There are a few steep stairs up into the dome.

Lucerne Valley · Saturday

Wolf Mountain

10 am

A small sanctuary that has cared for wolves since 1985. It's one of the few places where you can get close enough to touch one.

No hats, gloves or sunglasses around the wolves, and the guides take all the photos.

Pappy & Harriets · Saturday

Willi Carlisle

9:30 pm

An Ozarks folk-country storyteller on “The Universal Bubba Tour”, with Amelia Day opening. His album Peculiar, Missouri is the one to queue up on the drive out.

Pappy's has been a desert roadhouse since 1982, and it's a short walk from our room.

Pioneertown · Fri & Sat

Our Bunkhouse

2 nights

The Pioneertown Motel sits on a Western movie set built in 1946. There are no TVs, hammocks out back, and coffee in the Canteen every morning.

Pappy's, the Red Dog Saloon and Mane Street are all just a short walk away.
